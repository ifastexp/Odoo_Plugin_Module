iFAST Express

Same-day and next-day delivery across the UAE, connected to your Odoo.

Price at checkout

The iFAST Express shipping method gives the delivery price on quotations and on your website checkout, from your own iFAST prices.

Send when you validate

Validate a delivery and it is created in iFAST. Or select many deliveries and use Send to iFAST.

AWB labels and tracking

Print iFAST AWB labels from Odoo. The tracking number and link are saved on the delivery, with status notes in the chatter.

Cash on delivery

When the sale is not paid, iFAST collects the cash from your customer. Paid orders are delivered without collection.

Your iFAST console

One click opens iFAST: orders, live status and ETA, dashboard, finance and stock, in English and Arabic.

Odoo Online too

No module possible on your plan? Connect with an Odoo API key at connect5.ifast.express instead.

Inside your Odoo

iFAST price at website checkout

Your customer picks iFAST Express and sees your iFAST price for their address.

Send many deliveries at once

Select deliveries in Inventory and choose Actions > Send to iFAST.

AWB label and status on every delivery

The AWB PDF is attached, and each iFAST status (Collected, Out for delivery, Delivered) is noted with the tracking link.

Nothing is lost

If iFAST cannot take a delivery, it is still validated in Odoo and clearly marked Not sent to iFAST with the reason.

Connect in one click

Inventory > Configuration > Settings > iFAST Express.

Your iFAST console

Set up in three steps

  1. Install the module, then open Inventory > Configuration > Settings > iFAST Express and click Connect iFAST.
  2. In the iFAST page that opens, sign in with your iFAST shipper account (or ask iFAST for one at ifast.express).
  3. Use the iFAST Express shipping method on your sales, or send deliveries from the list.

You need an iFAST Express shipper account. The module itself is free. Support: support@ifast.express
